All students will adhere to the [code of conduct](https://policies.ncsu.edu/regulation/reg-11-35-05-code-of-student-conduct/) developed by NCSU, which pertains to both academic and non-academic misconduct. Students will also commit to in-person participation, with the exception of valid absences outlined by NCSU's [attendance regulations](https://policies.ncsu.edu/regulation/reg-02-20-03-attendance-regulations/). Becuase of the nature of paper discussions and presentations, make-up activities may not always be possible. Active participation and discussion is also expected during lectures. All discussion and questions will take place in good faith with a positive and supportive environment.
